ROOTED IN A REAL RIVER

The Baton Rouge riverfront.

The full East Baton Rouge Parish riverfront and the opposite bank, mapped in metres without compressing the route.

Riverbanks and the route use official EBRGIS hydrography. Scenery combines USGS aerial imagery and ground elevation with municipal building footprints and recorded heights. Bridges occupy their mapped crossings. The downtown riverfront adds a finer USGS elevation patch; other reaches retain the earlier terrain grid. The fixed water stage and historical source dates still limit shoreline accuracy. Building foundations follow the visible ground; unsupported generic footprints over water are omitted. Its red BATON ROUGE lettering, including the fleur-de-lis, is reconstructed at the location visible in public aerial imagery.

The location menu includes ExxonMobil’s refinery and L’Auberge Casino & Hotel. Custom models show the refinery’s mapped tanks and columns, the casino and hotel, Tiger Stadium’s bowl and upper decks, both capitols, and both river bridges. Drag to look around and scroll to zoom while the boat holds its inspection position.

USS KIDD appears at her usual berth in this historical riverfront view. The museum currently reports the ship away in Houma for repairs. Select USS KIDD in the location menu for a close river-side view.

Ground locations and route distances are geographically anchored. Bridge structures, façades, tree volumes, water stage, currents, and traffic remain modeled. This is not a certified 1:1 visual or navigation survey.
