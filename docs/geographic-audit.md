# Geographic audit — October 28, 2026

Geographic baseline: `5d0063b`. The audit covers the full **64,321.072 m** route, both banks, **13 consecutive reach maps and five detail maps**. It includes five supplementary 2025 reference panels, source research and building inventories. It does not certify every feature or a present-day shoreline.

## Coverage and interpretation

- **5,617 source buildings = 5,352 generic + 256 custom-model replacements + nine unsupported omissions.** Replacements count source records, not meshes. The bulk source is EBRGIS polygons intersecting a 1,600 m route buffer; no equivalent west-bank source is included. [Municipal layer](https://maps.brla.gov/gis/rest/services/Cadastral/Building_Footprint/MapServer/0), [metadata](https://city.brla.gov/gis/metadata/BUILDING.html).
- **10,654 Overture comparison records; 5,269 unmatched candidates; 1,792 within 500 m of mapped water boundary.** All unmatched candidates divide into **4,755 right-downstream / 514 left-downstream**, measured relative to the route, not parish boundaries. An unmatched result means best overlap below 20% of the smaller footprint area. Different epochs, segmentation and inference errors can cause it: these are **not verified missing buildings**.
- Overture release **2026-10-21.0** is audit-only comparison data and was not imported into game assets. Buildings are ODbL-licensed; retain per-feature provenance and **© OpenStreetMap contributors, Overture Maps Foundation** attribution with redistributed comparisons. [Official attribution](https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/).
- Source heights are absent/nonpositive for 685 records; 37 more are at most 1 m and also replaced. Emitted generic buildings include 66 floor-count models and 648 default 3.6 m models. The source ground-elevation datum is unverified.

The atlas's gold footprints represent raw game source records, including custom replacements and omissions. Pink is comparison screening, not a confirmed deficiency layer. Reach inventories are assigned by footprint centroid; the 35–55 km interval warrants the first detailed candidate review. Detail maps cover Port Allen, downtown/KIDD, US 190, L'Auberge and ID160843.

## Corrected omission: ID160843

The source polygon matches the **Belle riverboat access complex at 620 S River Road**, including a long covered riverward connection and connected landside masses. Site association comes from municipal address proximity; component interpretation comes from the aerial overlay. It is **not a confirmed missing ten-story hotel tower**. Approximately 87 m of its 670 m perimeter intersects game water, so the generic support rule omits the whole record despite a supported center. Obtain component heights/support before splitting it; do not apply the record's 92 ft / ten-floor attributes to the entire polygon. [Footprint query](https://maps.brla.gov/gis/rest/services/Cadastral/Building_Footprint/MapServer/0/query?where=OBJECTID%3D160843&outFields=*&returnGeometry=true&outSR=4326&f=pjson), [address query](https://maps.brla.gov/gis/rest/services/Map_Reference/Street_Address/MapServer/0/query?geometry=-91.190237754%2C30.442270997&geometryType=esriGeometryPoint&inSR=4326&spatialRel=esriSpatialRelIntersects&distance=100&units=esriSRUnit_Meter&outFields=*&returnGeometry=true&outSR=4326&f=pjson).

## Source and datum limits

Game geometry is EPSG:26915 metres, X east/Z south. Core aerial source date is **2023-05-03**. USDA comparison catalogues include **2025-05-14** and **2025-08-25**; newer mosaics supplement historical imagery rather than establish one scene epoch. Native resolution, export sampling and GPU upload resolution are different. [USGS game-imagery source](https://imagery.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/USGSNAIPPlus/ImageServer), [USDA comparison service](https://apps.geo.fpac.usda.gov/geo-imagery/rest/services/naip/conus_naip/ImageServer).

Most terrain retains a 13.8 m sampled DEM; the 2 m detail patch is local to downtown. Twelve both-bank spot checks returned 1 m 3DEP candidates, with metadata periods in 2018–2019 and 2021; this does not certify continuous coverage. Six NLD embankment features include east/west RTK survey dates in 2015/2017. Preserve CRS, vertical units, NAVD88 realization and survey dates. [3DEP service](https://elevation.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/3DEPElevation/ImageServer), [NLD embankments](https://geospatial.sec.usace.army.mil/dls/rest/services/NLD/Public/FeatureServer/11).

The mapped water layer has no stage/date or Z. Its 0.65 m simplification is a processing tolerance, not positional accuracy. Rendering and collision share the polygons, but this does not establish shoreline truth. The fixed 4 m gameplay water plane is not a historical observation. USACE's local Baton Rouge conversion is NAVD88 feet = gage feet − 0.71; 4 m would imply about 13.833 ft locally, without establishing any imagery-date stage or reach-wide slope. [Hydrography](https://maps.brla.gov/gis/rest/services/Hydrography/Hydrography/MapServer/0), [USACE gage](https://rivergages.mvr.usace.army.mil/WaterControl/stationinfo2.cfm?dt=E&sid=01160).

The base mesh intentionally adjusts 19,153 shoreline vertices to world Y=0.15 m. Downtown detail preserves the first 3 m and blends over another 17 m. Treat this as authored surface reconciliation. The 2,033 sampled dry-land support checks found no gaps; they are not a topographic survey. Depth/current/traffic remain models. USACE IENC starts at river mile 236; downtown/southern charting requires NOAA ENC and dated MVN surveys with their own depth references. [IENC](https://ienccloud.us/), [NOAA ENC](https://nauticalcharts.noaa.gov/charts/noaa-enc.html).

DOTD 2023 Metros 3-inch imagery is a sharper candidate: all 161 intersecting primary records reported no restrictions. DOTD 2025 6-inch records instead say restrictions apply despite generic service text; resolve rights before game redistribution. [2023 service](https://maps.dotd.la.gov/imagery/rest/services/Imagery/2023_Metros_3IN_RGBI/ImageServer), [2025 service](https://maps.dotd.la.gov/imagery/rest/services/Imagery/2025_Various_6IN_RGBI/ImageServer).

## Priorities and delivery design

Eight sourced west-bank/approach priorities are the deepwater wharf/transit sheds, Drax domes/shiploader, Louis Dreyfus grain elevator, Port Allen Lock/forebay, port administration/levee buildings, Old Ferry Landing/Centennial Park/City Hall, Brusly's Saint James Park, and review of the existing US 190 bridge approaches. These are research/modeling priorities, not approved geometries. [Official port layout](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/533972c5e4b0b7f04d8f67a2/t/5b719ee5cd83666edb4ec0e7/1534172901928/Deepwater%2BComplex%2BMap.pdf), [USACE lock](https://www.mvn.usace.army.mil/Media/Fact-Sheets/Fact-Sheet-Article-View/Article/3766067/port-allen-lock-reopens-after-usace-completes-repairs/), [Brusly park](https://www.bruslyla.com/saintjamespark/), [DOTD bridge diagram](https://dotd.la.gov/media/dbuegte2/mississippi-river-huey-p-long-bridge-160802a_051880.pdf).

Use Flight Simulator's layered source/fallback approach and screen-size-based LOD as design inspiration. First choose an epoch and retain provenance; verify west-bank candidates; extend source-selected near-bank terrain; then deliver bounded scenery chunks with coarse complete coverage and nearby landmark detail. Preserve collision geometry across visual LOD and measure startup/frame-time/memory before adding density. Existing terrain totals 1,122,007 triangles across 13 synchronously constructed chunks. This is not a promise of equivalent photogrammetry. [Microsoft world layers](https://docs.flightsimulator.com/html/Introduction/Using_The_SDK.htm), [LOD selection](https://docs.flightsimulator.com/msfs2024/flighting/models-and-textures/modeling/lods/lod-selection-system/).

## Implemented during the audit

External cameras now clear sampled terrain by 0.75 m before/after smoothing; ordinary helm attachment is preserved. Eleven targeted regressions pass, with independent 324 open-water comparisons and 11 actual terrain cases. This checks camera centers, not building collision or sightlines.

Quality switching now keeps the last usable image until replacement succeeds, preserves it after failure, permits retry and rejects stale callbacks. Thirteen imagery regressions and the lead's full suite passed. Local browser smoke checks and the frozen near-bank visual reproduction passed; public release verification is recorded separately. Continuous voyage, mobile and sustained GPU/frame-time checks remain separate work.

Six specialist roles worked in waves: three leads (geography/shoreline, buildings/landmarks, gameplay/performance), each with support (stage/charts, official landmark identification, independent camera QA). The coordinator produced the atlas and integrated the changes. Only the two narrowly verified presentation fixes were implemented; comparison geometry and speculative landmark masses remain outside the game.
